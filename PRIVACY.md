# sapf for iPhone and iPad — Privacy Policy

sapf does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- No accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, or tracking.
- The app makes no network connections.
- Everything you write, run, record, or save stays on your device, in the app's
  Documents folder (visible in the Files app). It is included in your device
  backups like any other app's documents, under your control.
- Microphone: if you use features that take live audio input, the sound is
  processed on your device as it plays. Anything you choose to record is saved
  only in the app's Documents folder. Audio is never sent anywhere or collected.
- The app does not use the camera, location, or contacts.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change is released.
